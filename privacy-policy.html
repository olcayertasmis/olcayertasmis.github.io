1. Scope & Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy governs all mobile games and applications (collectively referred to as the "Applications" or "Games") developed and published by YacloGames (operated by Olcay Ertaşmış), including but not limited to:

  • Paint Hit (Package ID: com.YacloGames.PaintHit) — Standalone offline casual game.
  • Ball Blasting — Arcade puzzle title featuring optional in-app purchases, Google Mobile Ads, and Google Play Games Services.
  • All future titles released by YacloGames on the Google Play Store.

We are dedicated to safeguarding user privacy and being transparent about our data handling practices.

2. Architecture-Specific Data Practices

A. Offline & Standalone Titles (e.g., Paint Hit)

Paint Hit is built as an offline casual title:

  • Does NOT collect any personal identity information (PII).
  • Does NOT use advertising networks or analytics trackers.
  • Stores level progress and scores purely locally on your personal device.

B. Connected Titles with Services (e.g., Ball Blasting)

In titles with integrated Google services and monetization (such as Ball Blasting), third-party services may process specific technical and gameplay data:

  • In-App Purchases (IAP): Processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. YacloGames never accesses, collects, or stores your credit card, bank details, or billing information. All financial transactions are managed entirely by Google LLC.
  • Advertisements (Google AdMob): Non-sensitive technical identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID and IP address) may be processed by Google to deliver, measure, and personalize ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Game Analytics & Crash Reporting (Firebase): We use Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) to measure game performance, monitor crash logs, analyze retention, and improve gameplay balance.
  • Leaderboards & Achievements (Google Play Games Services): If you choose to sign in with your Google Play Games account, your public gamer profile, scores, and unlocked achievements are synchronized with Google Play leaderboards.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Our applications may integrate trusted third-party SDKs provided by industry leaders. Each third-party provider operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase: Google Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies (Game Engine): Unity Player Privacy Policy

4. Device Permissions

Our games only request standard system permissions necessary for gameplay execution:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for online leaderboards, ad rendering, crash reporting, and Google Play licensing.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the game engine to determine whether an active internet connection is available.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Required for in-game purchases via Google Play.

We do NOT request access to your camera, microphone, contact list, GPS location, or external media files.

5. Opt-Out & Advertising ID Controls

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device. You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads anytime via:

Device Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policies)

Protecting children's privacy is of the utmost importance to YacloGames:

  • Our games comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
  • In games intended for mixed audiences or children, third-party advertising SDKs are configured to serve contextual, non-personalized ads appropriate for family audiences.
  • If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us at yaclogames@gmail.com, and we will take immediate corrective action.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

YacloGames does not operate proprietary backend databases storing personal user profiles. Gameplay progress tied to Google Play Games Services can be managed or deleted directly through the Google Play Games app. To reset local gameplay data, simply clear application data in your Android settings or uninstall the game.
